ABDOUL KARIM DIALLO, DIRECTOR GENERAL, SONOCO GROUP. Whereas, Mr. Abdoul Karim Diallo is an industrial pioneer who has reshaped the West African landscape by transforming a family-founded enterprise into a powerhouse conglomerate. After joining Sonoco in 2009, he pursued a hands-on path, mastering every facet of the industrial chain, from port logistics to metallurgy. His Harvard-trained strategic vision has allowed the Sonoco Group to evolve into a diverse engine of growth across multiple sectors; and Whereas, his most notable achievement is the 2019 US $25 million financing agreement with the IFC (World Bank), which modernized Guinean food processing and grain storage. This milestone has been critical in enhancing Guinea's food security and reducing reliance on imports; and Whereas, furthermore, his expansion into the beverage sector, including partnerships with global brands like Coca-Cola and Nestlé, has demonstrated the Sonoco Group's capacity to meet international quality standards; and Whereas, Mr. Diallo's impact is underscored by his advocacy for African-led industrialization. By digitalizing operations via SAP and attracting talent from the African diaspora, he has created a model of high-performance management; and Whereas, Mr. Diallo's recognition in the Choiseul 100 Africa highlights his role as a key contributor to the broader debate on economic sovereignty, proving that Africa has the capacity to build and thrive on its own strength. Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the House of Representatives: That the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives, by this resolution, honor the life and achievements of prominent African industrial leader Mr. Abdoul Karim Diallo, director general, Sonoco Group.
