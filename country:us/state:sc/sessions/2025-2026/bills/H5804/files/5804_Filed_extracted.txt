Whereas, the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives are delighted to learn that Sopharvathdy Vorn and Woods Wooten, son of our colleague the Honorable Chris Wooten, are to be wed on Sunday, September 20, 2026, at the Eternal Father of the Sea Chapel in North Charleston; and Whereas, Miss Vorn and Mr. Wooten earned bachelor's degrees from Wofford College: they are proud Terriers. His bachelor's degree was in government, and she earned a master's degree from Liberty University in counseling; and Whereas, the South Carolina House of Representatives wishes the couple a beautiful wedding day and a lifetime of joy as they become husband and wife. Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the House of Representatives: That the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives, by this resolution, congratulate Nixon Woods Wooten and offer best wishes to Sopharvathdy Vandy Vorn upon their upcoming nuptials. Be it further resolved that a copy of this resolution be presented to Sopharvathdy Vandy Vorn and Nixon Woods Wooten. ----XX---- This web page was last updated on August 25, 2026 at 1:55 PM
