A house RESOLUTION TO RECOGNIZE AND HONOR THE SALVATION ARMY OF AIKEN FOR A CENTURY OF FAITHFUL AND COMPASSIONATE SERVICE TO THE PEOPLE OF AIKEN COUNTY AND THE SURROUNDING REGION AND TO CONGRATULATE ITS OFFICERS, EMPLOYEES, VOLUNTEERS, SUPPORTERS, AND THOSE THEY SERVE, AS THE ORGANIZATION OBSERVES ITS ONE HUNDREDTH YEAR OF MINISTRY IN AIKEN. Whereas, the South Carolina House of Representatives is pleased to recognize the Salvation Army's enduring commitment to faith, service, and compassion that has strengthened these communities; and Whereas, The Salvation Army officially began its ministry on Union Street and later established a presence on Park Avenue in Aiken on January 16, 1927. Under the leadership of Envoys John and Thelma Gomillion, a ministry dedicated to serving individuals and families in need and sharing the hope of the Christian faith was established. The Salvation Army quickly became an important part of the Aiken community and began building a tradition of service that would span generations; and Whereas, throughout the ensuing century, devoted Salvation Army officers, employees, volunteers, donors, advisory board members, community partners, and other supporters have given generously of their time, talents, and resources to improve the lives of their neighbors; and Whereas, through changing times and changing community needs, The Salvation Army of Aiken has remained steadfast in its calling to provide emergency assistance, disaster response, food, shelter, clothing, and countless other acts of compassion to those facing hardship, as well as sponsoring the annual Angel Tree to ensure families and children have a merry Christmas; and Whereas, today The Salvation Army continues this proud tradition through the Red Shield Emergency Shelter, feeding programs, Emergency Social Services, the Red Shield Youth Center, disaster relief, thrift stores, worship ministries, and programs that touch thousands of people each year; and Whereas, its ministry now reaches beyond Aiken County to serve individuals and families throughout Aiken, Allendale, Barnwell, and Edgefield counties by extending the organization's influence and compassionate care across a significant portion of western South Carolina; and Whereas, generations of Aiken residents have known The Salvation Army as a place where those confronting hunger, homelessness, financial hardship, disaster, loneliness, or spiritual need may find a helping hand, a caring heart, and renewed hope. Its century of service reflects The Salvation Army's enduring mission to preach the gospel of Jesus Christ and meet human needs in His name without partiality, a mission carried forward in Aiken through practical service grounded in Christian love; and Whereas, as The Salvation Army of Aiken observes its one hundredth year of ministry, its leaders and supporters are not only celebrating an extraordinary legacy, but also looking toward the future with renewed vision through innovative ministries, stronger community partnerships, and plans for a future Center of Hope campus designed to expand its ability to serve generations yet to come; and Whereas, the South Carolina House of Representatives deeply appreciates the great contributions The Salvation Army has made to Aiken and beyond since 1927 and recognizes its remarkable record of service has been made possible by generations of faithful officers, employees, volunteers, donors, churches, businesses, civic organizations, community partners, and friends desiring to serve others. It is altogether fitting that the House of Representatives should pause to celebrate this historic milestone and express the gratitude of the people of this State for a century of faith in action. Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the House of Representatives: That the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives, by this resolution, recognize and honor The Salvation Army of Aiken for a century of faithful and compassionate service to the people of Aiken County and the surrounding region and congratulate its officers, employees, volunteers, supporters, community partners, and those they serve, as the organization observes its one hundredth year of ministry in Aiken. Be it further resolved that a copy of this resolution be presented to The Salvation Army of Aiken. ----XX---- This web page was last updated on August 11, 2026 at 03:21 PM
