Whereas, Mrs. Vanester Evans has been a faithful and devoted member of Saint Paul African Methodist Episcopal Church for fifty years, demonstrating unwavering commitment to the mission and ministry of the church; and Whereas, throughout her years of dedicated service, she has faithfully served in numerous leadership and ministry roles, including class leader, steward, church treasurer, president of the jubilee choir, and in several other capacities, generously sharing her time, talents, wisdom, and gifts for the advancement of the church's congregation; and Whereas, Mrs. Evans has extended her servant leadership beyond the walls of the church by serving as chairperson of the Chestnut Consolidated High School organization for the past twenty-five years, providing outstanding leadership and steadfast support to educational, historical, and community initiatives; and Whereas, she has further exemplified excellence in civic and fraternal service through her faithful leadership as Worthy Matron of Moonlight Chapter No. 168, discharging her duties with dignity, integrity, compassion, and unwavering commitment to the principals of the Order of the Eastern Star; and Whereas, her life of faithful service, exemplary leadership, Christian character, and selfless dedication has been a source of inspiration to countless individuals, enriching both Saint Paul African Methodist Episcopal Church and the greater community she so faithfully serves. Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the House of Representatives: That the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives, by this resolution, recognize and honor Vanester Evans for her enduring commitment, exceptional leadership, unwavering faith and exemplary service to Saint Paul African Methodist Episcopal Church. Be it further resolved that a copy of this resolution be presented to Mrs. Vanester Evans. ----XX---- This web page was last updated on August 11, 2026 at 3:03 PM
