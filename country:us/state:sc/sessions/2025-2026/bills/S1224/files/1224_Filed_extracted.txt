Whereas, Positive Vibes Ronjanae Smith Inc. was established in loving memory of Ronjanae Smith, the 14-year-old daughter of founder Ronald Smith, whose life was tragically taken by gun violence in May 2021; and Whereas, through unimaginable loss, Ronald Smith transformed grief into purpose by creating an organization dedicated to educating, empowering, and engaging communities in the fight against gun violence while providing hope and healing, as well as offering programs designed to give individuals and families the tools and resources they need to thrive; and Whereas, for the past five years, Positive Vibes Ronjanae Smith Inc. has demonstrated unwavering leadership and compassion by offering mentorship programs, conflict resolution training, mental health resources, grief support, workforce development opportunities, educational initiatives, youth engagement, and community outreach designed to strengthen neighborhoods and improve lives; and Whereas, the organization has become a trusted advocate for violence prevention by fostering partnerships with community leaders, educators, businesses, faith organizations, law enforcement, and public officials to advance collaborative solutions that create safer and stronger communities; and Whereas, Ronald Smith has dedicated himself to ensuring that Ronjanae's legacy continues through service, advocacy, and prevention, inspiring countless individuals to resolve conflict peacefully, invest in young people, and work collectively to reduce gun violence throughout the Lowcountry and beyond; and Whereas, through the establishment of Positive Vibes Interrupter to Violence, Mr. Smith has further expanded his commitment to implementing innovative, community-led strategies that address the root causes of violence while promoting healing, accountability, and opportunity; and Whereas, on Saturday, July 18, 2026, the Sneaker Legacy & Culture Gala marked the fifth anniversary of Positive Vibes Ronjanae Smith Inc. under the theme "Celebrating 5 years of Impact. Building the Next 5 years," recognizing the organization's remarkable accomplishments and reaffirming its commitment to creating lasting change for future generations; and Whereas, Positive Vibes Ronjanae Smith Inc. has become a beacon of hope by promoting violence prevention, fostering positive opportunities for youth and families, and inspiring community engagement that strengthens neighborhoods and improves the quality of life for all citizens.  Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the Senate: That the members of the South Carolina Senate, by this resolution, congratulate Positive Vibes Ronjanae Smith Inc. upon the occasion of its fifth anniversary and commend the organization on its outstanding commitment to gun violence prevention, community empowerment, and public service. Be it further resolved that a copy of this resolution be presented to Mr. Ronald Smith. ----XX---- This web page was last updated on August 11, 2026 at 12:31 PM
