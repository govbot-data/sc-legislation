A house RESOLUTION To recognize and honor the Right Reverend Suffragan Bishop William Robert Cue and First Lady Karen Cue for forty years of outstanding Christian leadership, faithful pastoral ministry, and dedicated service to the people of Trenton, Edgefield County, and the State of South Carolina. Whereas, in 1986, Bishop William Robert Cue and First Lady Karen Cue founded New & Living Way Apostolic Church with a vision to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and serve the spiritual needs of their community; and Whereas, during forty years of ministry, they have faithfully shepherded generations of families through biblical teaching, prayer, worship, and Christian discipleship; and Whereas, their ministry has extended beyond the church walls through community outreach, support for families, mentoring young people, and encouraging unity throughout Edgefield County and the surrounding region; and Whereas, Bishop Cue has demonstrated unwavering leadership, integrity, and servant-hearted ministry while faithfully answering God's call to preach the Gospel; and Whereas, First Lady Karen Cue has faithfully served alongside her husband with grace, compassion, wisdom, and dedication, strengthening the ministry and touching countless lives through her leadership and service. Together they have left a lasting legacy of faith, hope, and love that continues to bless their congregation and the citizens of South Carolina; and Whereas, the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives are thankful to Bishop William Robert Cue and First Lady Karen Cue for their faithful service to the people of Edgefield County and extend their sincere appreciation and best wishes for God's continued blessings upon them and the ministry of New & Living Way Apostolic Church. Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the House of Representatives: That the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives, by this resolution, recognize and honor the Right Reverend Suffragan Bishop William Robert Cue and First Lady Karen Cue for forty years of outstanding Christian leadership, faithful pastoral ministry, and dedicated service to the people of Trenton, Edgefield County, and the State of South Carolina. Be it further resolved that a copy of this resolution be presented to the Right Reverend Suffragan Bishop William Robert Cue and First Lady Karen Cue. ----XX---- This web page was last updated on August 11, 2026 at 03:36 PM
