A house RESOLUTION TO CONGRATULATE RHIANNON WYATT OF PAULINE-GLENN SPRINGS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL UPON BEING NAMED 2026 SPARTANBURG COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT 6 TEACHER OF THE YEAR, TO EXPRESS APPRECIATION FOR HER DEDICATED SERVICE TO CHILDREN, AND TO WISH HER CONTINUED SUCCESS IN THE FUTURE. Whereas, the South Carolina House of Representatives is pleased to learn that Rhiannon Wyatt has been selected 2026 Teacher of the Year for Spartanburg County School District 6; and Whereas, a K-5 kindergarten teacher at Pauline-Glenn Springs Elementary School, Mrs. Wyatt has served as an educator for twenty-five years. In preparation for her career, she earned a bachelor's degree in education from the University of South Carolina Upstate and a master's degree in special education from Converse College, as well as National Board Certification. As a teacher, she finds invaluable support in the encouragement of her family: her husband, Daniel; and their two children, Jackson and Julia; and Whereas, each district Teacher of the Year in the Palmetto State undergoes a rigorous screening and assessment process in order to be selected as the top teacher in that district; and Whereas, representing all teachers in South Carolina, these teachers act as role models and mentors for their colleagues in their districts and demonstrate a consistent commitment to excellence; and Whereas, district Teachers of the Year have hundreds of years of combined teaching experience and possess a proven ability to make a positive difference for their students, schools, and communities; and Whereas, South Carolina is fortunate to have such dedicated and caring teachers educating and molding South Carolina's youth; and Whereas, it is entirely appropriate for the South Carolina House of Representatives to pause in its deliberations to honor Rhiannon Wyatt of Pauline-Glenn Springs Elementary School, 2026 Teacher of the Year for Spartanburg County School District 6, for amply providing her students the opportunity to live complete and successful lives as future leaders of their communities. Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the House of Representatives: That the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives, by this resolution, congratulate Rhiannon Wyatt of Pauline-Glenn Springs Elementary School upon being named 2026 Spartanburg County School District 6 Teacher of the Year, express appreciation for her dedicated service to children, and wish her continued success in the future. Be it further resolved that a copy of this resolution be presented to Rhiannon Wyatt. ----XX---- This web page was last updated on August 11, 2026 at 03:10 PM
