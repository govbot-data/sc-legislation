Whereas, the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives are pleased to commend Aristidis Anagnostaras-Adams, whose visionary leadership and professional achievements have made a profound impact on the development of global mining and finance; and Whereas, Mr. Anagnostaras-Adams has dedicated his career to bridging international capital with African industrialization; and Whereas, a former chartered accountant and top-tier Master of Business Administration graduate, Mr. Anagnostaras-Adams spent two decades with the founding family of Brake Horsepower (BHP), before spearheading KEFI's expansion. His leadership is defined by a "future-building" philosophy that emphasizes transparency and shareholder-aligned growth; and Whereas, KEFI's flagship Tulu Kapi Gold project in Ethiopia stands as his most significant milestone, recently securing a US $340 million financing package. This project is widely recognized for its adherence to rigorous environmental, social, and technical governance (ESG) standards, serving as a regional blueprint for responsible resource extraction. By modernizing mining infrastructure in Ethiopia and Saudi Arabia, he has provided a tangible path for industrial self-reliance; and Whereas, Mr. Anagnostaras-Adams is a champion of multicultural integration in business. His ability to navigate the complexities of international bear markets while maintaining unwavering standards has helped restore global investor confidence in African mining. His work continues to catalyze secondary economic development in local communities, creating jobs and fostering long-term regional economic stability. Now, therefore, Be it resolved by the House of Representatives: That the members of the South Carolina House of Representatives, by this resolution, honor and recognize Aristidis "Harry" Anagnostaras-Adams, executive chairman of KEFI Gold and Copper Programmable Logic Controller (PLC), for his transformative work in global mining and finance.
